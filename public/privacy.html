2. Personal data we collect

We collect personal data that you give us directly. This includes your name, email address, phone number, company name and postal address, the details of any account you create, and any messages, enquiries or feedback you send us. If you buy something from us, we also receive transaction details such as the items purchased, the amount paid, the payment method used and the date. Where we take card payments, the full card details are handled by our payment providers, and we do not keep complete card numbers ourselves.

We also collect information automatically when you use the Services. This includes your IP address, browser type and version, device type and operating system, the pages or screens you view, the date and time of your visits, the page that referred you, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. We collect much of this through cookies and similar technologies, which are explained in section 6.

We may also receive personal data from third parties. Examples include payment providers confirming a transaction, analytics providers, sign-in services if you choose to log in with an account from another provider, and public sources where the law allows.

3. How we use your personal data

We use personal data to provide, operate and maintain the Services; to create and manage your account; to process orders, payments and refunds and to send receipts and invoices; to respond to your enquiries and provide customer support; to send you service messages such as confirmations, security alerts and changes to these policies; to improve and personalise the Services and understand how they are used; to detect, prevent and respond to fraud, abuse and security incidents; to keep the records required by tax, accounting and other laws; and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

The Data Protection Act, 2019 (the “Act”) requires us to have a lawful basis for each use of personal data. Depending on the purpose, our lawful bases are your consent, where we ask for it, such as for marketing messages or non-essential cookies; the performance of a contract with you, or steps you ask us to take before entering into a contract; compliance with a legal obligation, such as keeping tax records; and our legitimate interests, such as running and securing our business, answering enquiries and improving the Services. Where we rely on legitimate interests, we balance them against your rights and freedoms before we proceed.

4. Marketing

We send marketing messages only where you have opted in or where the law otherwise allows it, and you can object at any time. Every marketing message will include a simple way to unsubscribe. You can also withdraw your consent by contacting us, which will not affect the lawfulness of anything done before you withdrew it. Service messages, such as receipts and security notices, will continue to be sent even if you opt out of marketing.

5. Who we share personal data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only where it is needed. We share it with service providers who process data on our behalf to run the Services, such as cloud hosting, email delivery, payment processing, analytics and customer support tools, and these providers are required by contract to protect the data and use it only on our instructions. We share it with professional advisers such as lawyers, accountants and auditors, who are bound by duties of confidentiality. We share it with government bodies, regulators, courts or law enforcement where the law requires it or where disclosure is necessary to protect rights, property or safety. If we sell or reorganise all or part of our business, we may transfer personal data to the buyer or successor, subject to the same protections. We also share personal data with anyone you direct us to share it with, or where you have otherwise consented.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies. Essential cookies are needed for the Services to work, for example to keep you signed in, and cannot be switched off without affecting how the Services function. Analytics and similar cookies help us understand how people use the Services. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent before setting non-essential cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings, and most browsers let you block or delete them. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the Services from working properly.

7. International transfers

Some of our service providers store or process personal data outside Kenya, for example on cloud servers located in other countries. We transfer personal data outside Kenya only where the Act permits it, such as where appropriate safeguards are in place, including binding contractual commitments from the recipient, or where you have given your consent. We take reasonable steps to make sure your data remains protected wherever it is processed.

8. How long we keep personal data

We keep personal data only for as long as it is needed for the purposes set out in this policy. Account information is generally kept while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards, so that we can respond to queries and resolve any disputes. Transaction and financial records are kept for the period required by Kenyan tax and accounting law. Enquiries and support correspondence are kept for as long as needed to resolve them and for a reasonable period afterwards. When personal data is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymise it so that it can no longer identify you.

9. How we keep personal data secure

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, alteration and disclosure. These include encrypting data in transit using HTTPS, storing passwords in hashed form, restricting access to staff who need it for their roles, and reviewing our security practices from time to time. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to result in a risk to you, we will notify you and the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner as the Act requires, including within 72 hours of becoming aware of a notifiable breach.

10. Children

The Services are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 without the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal data without that consent, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

11. Your rights

Under the Act, you have the right to be informed about how your personal data is used, which this policy is designed to do. You also have the right to access the personal data we hold about you; to ask us to correct data that is inaccurate, false or misleading; to ask us to delete such data; to object to our processing of your data, including processing for direct marketing; to receive your data in a portable format where the Act provides for it; to withdraw consent at any time where we rely on it; and not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that significantly affects you, except where the law allows it.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 1. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and we will respond within the time limits set by the Act. If you believe we have not handled your personal data in line with the Act, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner at odpc.go.ke. We would welcome the chance to address your concern first, but you are free to contact the Commissioner directly.
